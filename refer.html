How it works

  1. Get your link

    Sign in with your email. Your personal code, link, and QR code are ready in seconds.

  2. Share it

    Text it, post it, or let a friend scan the QR code from your phone.

  3. You both save

    When your friend's first visit is confirmed, their welcome credit is added. When it's done, yours is. A few friends can cover a whole clean.

Got a code from a friend? Open their link, or enter the code on the quote form.

The fine print, in plain words

  • Credit is for new customers: your friend must not have had a visit with us before.
  • Your friend's credit is added when we confirm their first visit. Yours is added when that visit is done.
  • Credit stacks with no limit and doesn't expire. It comes off the price of a visit when we confirm it, up to {maxPct} of that visit's price. It can't be paid out as cash or moved to another account.
  • You can't refer yourself, your household, or your own business, and we may remove credit earned that way.
  • We may change or end the program at any time. Credit you've already earned stays yours.
