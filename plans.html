How the estimate is built

Home size
Each plan starts from a base price and adds an amount per bedroom and per bathroom. Essentials skips bedrooms, so only bathrooms change its price.
Schedule discount
Booking monthly takes 5% off every visit, every 2 weeks takes 10% off, and weekly takes 15% off. It applies to the full per-visit price and stays as long as the schedule does.
What can change the final price
Unusually large rooms, heavy build-up that needs a deep clean first, or extras you ask for. If anything moves the number, you hear about it in the quote reply, never on the invoice.
