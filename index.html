Questions people ask first

How does the recurring discount work?
Each plan has a full per-visit price based on your home's bedrooms and bathrooms. {discountSentence} The discount stays as long as the schedule does.
Can I skip a week or pause?
Yes. Let us know before your visit and we'll skip it. If you pause for a while and come back, your plan and discount pick up where they left off.
Do I need to be home?
No. Most clients give us a way in: a lockbox, a door code, or a neighbor. We'll sort out what works for you when we confirm your first visit.
Do you bring supplies and equipment?
Yes. If you'd rather we use specific products in your home, leave them out and add a note to your plan.
How is the price set?
By the plan you choose and the size of your home. Bedrooms and bathrooms are the biggest factors. If the home needs a deep clean first, we'll quote that separately so the plan price stays predictable.
Where do you work?
